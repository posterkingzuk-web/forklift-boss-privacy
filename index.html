Forklift Boss: Warehouse Sim — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Forklift Boss: Warehouse Sim ("the game") is made by The Media Work ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos. The game does not include analytics or tracking of its own.

Your game progress

Your progress, coins, settings and unlocks are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play (on Android) or Apple's App Store (on iOS). We never see or store your payment card details. To confirm and restore purchases, the game may use RevenueCat, a purchase-verification service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt. It does not receive your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one, for example to double a shift's pay. There are no pop-up, banner or forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Account > Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses data in ads.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Data is only shared with the service providers named above, for the purposes described.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information on our own servers, there is normally nothing for us to access or delete. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings, and change ad consent in the game's settings. For any privacy question, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
